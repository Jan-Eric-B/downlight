# 05: The bar's buttons in full screen

**What to build:** On the **Title bar** of a **Full screen** window, the maximize button reads "Leave full screen" and leaves full screen, without bringing up Windows' snap layouts, and the bar's empty strip does nothing: no drag, no double-click, no window menu. Minimize and close work as usual.

Spec: [Full screen](../spec.md)

**Blocked by:** 01 (F11 and the menu switch full screen) for the page tests; 04 (The title bar slides in at the top edge) for the hand check, which needs a bar the reader can see

**Status:** built, hand checks left

- [x] In full screen the maximize button is named "Leave full screen", its tooltip is "Leave full screen (F11)", it shows the restore glyph and it runs the full screen command. Out of full screen it is maximize or restore as today.
- [x] While the window is full screen, the app stops answering the pointer over that button as the system's maximize button, so no snap layouts flyout comes; out of full screen it answers it as today.
- [x] While the window is full screen, the bar is no drag region: a drag, a double-click and a right-click on its strip do nothing. Out of full screen they are as today.
- [x] Page tests (written first): in full screen the button's name, tooltip and glyph are Leave full screen's, and a click asks the app to switch full screen; leaving brings maximize or restore back; the strip is no drag region in full screen and is one again after.

## Comments

**2026-11-20, built; the hand check is left.** What was built, and what it decided:

- **The button (`startTitleBar`, `page/src/title-bar.ts`).** One function, `showMaximizeButton`, names the button from two states: full screen, which the `whenFullScreenChanges` listener sets, and maximized, which the `window-state` message sets. Full screen goes first, since tao keeps a maximized window maximized underneath and the app goes on telling `maximized` during full screen (ticket 01). In full screen the button is "Leave full screen", its tooltip "Leave full screen (F11)" and its glyph the restore glyph; leaving brings back Restore or Maximize from the last window state. The tooltip takes F11 from the commands table through a new `tooltipOf(id, label)` in `page/src/commands.ts`, which `commandButton` now uses too.
- **The click.** The button stays the `maximize` command's: its `run` switches full screen while `isFullScreen()` and asks for `toggle_maximize_window` otherwise. `maximize` has no shortcut or menu entry, so only the button changes. Full screen's command and the maximize command share `switchFullScreenShown` (switch, then `refreshCommands`).
- **No snap layouts: the hit test reads the window's own style.** `hit_test` in `app/src/platform/windows/title_bar.rs` now takes the window's `Frame` (its resize border and whether it has a maximize box, read each time with `GetWindowLongPtrW(GWL_STYLE)`), and answers `HTTRANSPARENT` over the button when the window has no `WS_MAXIMIZEBOX`. tao 0.37 clears `WS_OVERLAPPEDWINDOW`, the maximize box included, while a window is borderless full screen (`WindowFlags::to_window_styles`, applied on the main thread before the window is sized to the monitor) and puts it back on leaving; Microsoft's snap layouts page says Windows shows them for a window whose maximize button is available. So the pointer goes through to the page's button, as it does beside it, the button's window gets no non-client messages (no hover look, no maximize on a click), and the state is the window's own: no flag in the app, no message from the page, nothing that can drift from what the window is. Chosen over the full screen command telling the platform module (a second copy of tao's state) and over the page withdrawing the button's place (the page's last answer, and the bar's `ResizeObserver` doesn't fire on the class change, ticket 03). No change to `switch_full_screen` or to any capability.
- **The strip.** `.title-bar.full-screen { app-region: no-drag; }` in `title-bar.css`, for the whole of full screen, shown or not; ticket 03's `no-drag` on `.away` is gone, as `.away` only comes in full screen. With no drag region WebView2 no longer drags, maximizes on a double-click or opens the window menu there; a double-click reaches a bar with nothing on it. A right-click would reach the page's own right-click menu instead, so the bar takes a `contextmenu` on its strip (anything not a `.bar-item`, the top zone included) in full screen: `preventDefault` and `stopPropagation`, so no menu opens. The bar's items keep what a right-click does on them today.
- **Tests** (written first): `page/src/title-bar.test.ts`: `in full screen the maximize button is Leave full screen, with the restore glyph and a tooltip naming F11`; `a click on Leave full screen asks the app to switch full screen, not to maximize`; `in full screen the window's maximized state leaves the button Leave full screen`; `leaving full screen brings back restore for a maximized window, and maximize for any other`; `in full screen the strip is no drag region, and leaving makes it one again`; `in full screen a right-click on the strip opens no menu`. The first three and the right-click one failed first. The leaving one passed from the start (the `window-state` path already set the label; it guards the new order). The drag one passed from the start too: happy-dom computes no `app-region`, so it reads the `full-screen` class the stylesheet keys `no-drag` on (new helper `titleBarDragsTheWindow()` in `page/src/test-support.ts`), and that class came with ticket 04; the stylesheet rule is what changed, and the hand check confirms it. `cargo test`: `full_screen_window_has_no_maximize_button_and_leaves_the_pointer_to_the_page` (failed first, to compile); the other hit test tests now pass a restored or maximized `Frame`.
- **Not done:** the hand check in the release build. If the button looked hovered when the window went full screen (pointer on maximize while pressing F11), the app's `WM_NCMOUSELEAVE` should still set it back to normal; the hand check confirms it.

**2026-11-20, review fixes (the page's side).**

- **Bug: the maximize button's place after leaving.** The window's resize back could lay the bar out while it was still `.away` (translated up), and nothing placed the button again once `.away` went, so snap layouts could miss the button. Leaving full screen now places it again once the classes are gone. In full screen the app lets the pointer through, so entering places nothing. Test (failed first): `leaving full screen tells the app where the maximize button is once the bar is back, though the window's resize laid it out while away`.
- **The flyout's CSS.** Back to `--title-bar-height` for `top` and `max-height`, with no `:has()` rule: the open flyout keeps the bar in view, so it always hangs below the bar's height (ticket 04's decision, unchanged).
- **One window switch module.** `page/src/window-switch.ts` (`windowSwitch(command, { whenSwitchedOn })`) holds the state, the starts counter, the switch that keeps only the answer for the current start, and the listeners; `keep-on-top.ts` and `full-screen.ts` are thin and keep their exports. Both fail the same way now: a failed switch says why and leaves the state.
- **One place knows full screen.** `title-bar.ts` reads `isFullScreen()` and keeps no copy. `followFullScreen(bar, maximize, signal)` holds the bar's full screen behaviour (the classes, the room, the top zone, the strip's right-click and the re-placing), and the signal's abort removes the inline `--title-bar-room`.
- **`maximize` stays maximize.** The title bar's maximize button is its own (`createMaximizeButton`), and runs `fullScreen` or `maximize` through the new `runCommand(id)` in `commands.ts`. `switchFullScreenShown` is gone, so F11, Esc, the menu and the button all go through `commands.fullScreen.run()`.
- **Tests.** The title bar's full screen tests moved to `page/src/title-bar-full-screen.test.ts`, with the setup they share with `title-bar.test.ts` in `test-support.ts` (`openWithTitleBar`, `closeTitleBarWindow`, `shownFrom`), and `menuEntryOf` replaces the copied menu lookups. Deleted: `in full screen the strip is no drag region, and leaving makes it one again` and `titleBarDragsTheWindow()`, which read the same class the page sets; the drag region stays in the hand check. Added: `a title bar stopped in full screen leaves the room at the top to the stylesheet`, `in full screen a right-click on the bar's items opens the page's menu, as out of full screen`, `out of full screen the title bar leaves a right-click on its strip alone`, and `Esc with the settings flyout open and the focus outside it leaves full screen alone` in `full-screen.test.ts`, which fails without Esc's flyout check. A click on the flyout where nothing takes the focus leaves the flyout open with the focus outside it. Esc closes nothing then, as the flyout hears Esc only with the focus inside it.
