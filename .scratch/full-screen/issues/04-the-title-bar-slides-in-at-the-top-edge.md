# 04: The title bar slides in at the top edge

**What to build:** In **Full screen** the hidden **Title bar** slides in over the document while the pointer rests at the top edge of the screen, and slides away once the reader is done with it. It lies over the document, so the reading position never moves, and covers the Find bar and the settings flyout while it shows. Its logo, file name (with its path and **Counts** tooltip, and reveal on a click), zoom level and buttons work as outside full screen.

Spec: [Full screen](../spec.md)

**Blocked by:** 03 (The title bar hides in full screen)

**Status:** built, hand checks left

- [x] In full screen, the bar shows while any of these holds: the pointer is in a thin zone at the top edge of the screen or over the bar, the focus is within the bar, or the settings flyout is open. It hides once none holds. Its tooltips are the system's and show only with the pointer over the bar, which that rule covers.
- [x] Shown, the bar keeps its own height and lies over the document; the room kept for it stays zero, so nothing under it moves.
- [x] It slides in and out, and appears and disappears at once when the system asks for reduced motion.
- [x] Out of full screen the bar is as today, whatever the pointer or the focus does.
- [x] Page tests (written first): in full screen the pointer entering the top zone shows the bar and leaving the zone and the bar hides it; the focus moving into the bar shows it and out hides it; the open settings flyout keeps it, and closing the flyout with the pointer and focus elsewhere hides it; the room kept for the bar stays zero while it shows.

## Comments

**2026-11-20, built; the hand check is left.** What was built, and what it decided:

- **What keeps the bar in view (`keepInViewWhileReached` in `page/src/title-bar.ts`).** The bar gets the class `full-screen` for as long as the window is full screen, and `away` while it is full screen and none of these holds: the pointer is over the bar (`pointerenter` and `pointerleave` on the bar), the focus is in it (`focusin`, and `focusout` whose `relatedTarget` is outside it, as the Notice does), or the settings flyout is open. The pointer and the focus are followed all the time, so leaving full screen and coming back finds them right; out of full screen the bar is never away. The room stays `0px` the whole time in full screen, so nothing under the bar moves.
- **The top zone is a part of the bar.** A `div.top-zone` (aria-hidden), the bar's last child, absolutely placed at `top: 100%`, 2 px high, `app-region: no-drag`, shown only in full screen. While the bar is away it is the screen's top 2 px; the pointer entering it is the pointer entering the bar, so it stays over the bar throughout the slide and after, with no zone to leave on the way (an element of its own would hand the pointer from the zone to a bar that is still moving, which Chromium tells only on the next pointer move). While the bar shows, the zone hangs below it and gives the pointer 2 px more before the bar slides away.
- **The slide (`title-bar.css`).** `.title-bar.away` is now `transform: translateY(-100%)` instead of ticket 03's `visibility: hidden`, so the bar can take the focus and Tab reaches its buttons; slid off the screen it covers nothing there, and it stays `app-region: no-drag`. `.title-bar.full-screen` has a transition of `transform 167ms ease-out` only under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, so with reduced motion it comes and goes at once; leaving full screen drops the transition with the class, so the bar is back at once. In full screen the bar draws the page's solid background (`light-dark(#ffffff, #0d1117)`, Canvas in high contrast), as the Find bar does, since it now lies over the document's text. Its z-index (4) is above the Find bar's (3), so it covers the Find bar while it shows.
- **The flyout announces opening and closing.** `whenSettingsFlyoutToggles(listener)` in `page/src/settings-flyout.ts`, shaped like `whenFullScreenChanges`, runs on open and on close; on close it runs after the focus has gone back to the gear, so a bar that keeps that focus in view never slides away in between.
- **A decision against the spec's letter: the flyout hangs below the bar in full screen.** The flyout keeps the bar in view for as long as it is open (story 20), so with story 47's flyout "at the top of the screen" the bar would always cover its top 28 px (the Theme legend and part of its choices). In full screen the flyout's `top` and `max-height` now use the bar's own height (`:root:has(> body > .title-bar.full-screen) .settings-flyout` in `settings-flyout.css`), so it hangs from the bar, where the gear is. It never moves while it shows, since the bar shows the whole time, which is story 48's point. The Find bar stays at the top of the screen and is covered while the bar slides in, as the spec says.
- **The focus on the gear keeps the bar.** Closing the flyout with Esc or the gear gives the focus back to the gear, so the bar stays until the focus leaves it (a click in the document), as story 19 asks: the reader never has a focused button they can't see. A click on the document closes the flyout and leaves the focus there, so the bar slides away.
- **No delay** before the bar slides in or away: the zone is only the screen's edge, which the pointer reaches on purpose. The hand check says whether a short one is wanted.
- **Tests** (`page/src/title-bar.test.ts`): `in full screen the pointer at the top edge shows the title bar, and leaving the zone and the bar hides it`; `in full screen the focus moving into the title bar shows it, and moving out hides it`; `in full screen the open settings flyout keeps the title bar, and closing it with the pointer and the focus elsewhere hides it`; `in full screen Ctrl+, opens the settings flyout with the title bar in view, and Esc leaves the bar to the focus on its gear`; `in full screen the title bar slides in over the document: the page keeps no room for it while it shows`; `out of full screen the title bar shows whatever the pointer and the focus do`. New helpers in `page/src/test-support.ts`: `titleBarTopZone()` and `movePointer(from, to)`, which dispatches `pointerleave` and `pointerenter` on each element left and entered, as the browser does and happy-dom doesn't. The file's fake app answers `editor_choices` now, for the flyout.
- **Not checked visually:** the page needs the Tauri app, so the slide, the zone and the z-order are left to the hand check. The Counts in the file name's tooltip aren't built yet; the tooltip shows the path.
- **For ticket 05.** The class `full-screen` stays on the bar for the whole of full screen, shown or not: `.title-bar.full-screen { app-region: no-drag; }` makes the strip no drag region, and ticket 03's `app-region: no-drag` on `.away` can go then. `keepInViewWhileReached`'s returned function is where the bar learns of full screen; the maximize button's name, tooltip and glyph can follow the same `whenFullScreenChanges` listener.
