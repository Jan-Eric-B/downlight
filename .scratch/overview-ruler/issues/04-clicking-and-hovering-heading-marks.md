# 04: Clicking and hovering heading marks

**What to build:** A click on a heading mark of the **Overview ruler** goes to that heading, as a heading link does, so Back returns to where the reader was. Hovering a heading mark names the heading before the reader clicks.

Spec: [Overview ruler](../spec.md)

**Blocked by:** 01 (Heading marks beside the scroll bar)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [x] A click in the strip's left half within a few pixels of a heading mark goes to the first heading of that mark's run through the view's `visitHeading`: it opens collapsed sections, scrolls as a heading link does and joins **History**.
- [x] A click on the strip where no mark is near does nothing.
- [x] Hovering a heading mark shows a label left of the strip, in the link target label's style, with the run's headings' text, up to five and then "and N more"; none over match marks or empty strip. The label goes when the pointer leaves.
- [x] The pointer is a hand over a mark.
- [x] Page tests (written first): a click on a heading mark goes there and Back returns; a click on a merged mark goes to its first heading; a click on empty strip leaves the view and History as they were; the hover label lists the run's headings.
- [x] `npm run check` passes (its Prettier step fails only on the stray `.claude/worktrees/` copy, not ours; every other step passes, and Prettier passes on the repo's own files).

## Hand checks

For the owner, in the built app; the page tests can't see these:

- [ ] The hover label sits just left of the strip, level with its mark, in the link target label's look, in both themes and in high contrast, and stays in the window for a mark near the bottom.
- [ ] The pointer is a hand over a mark (in the margin of a wide window and over the document's right padding of a narrow one) and the arrow beside the marks.
- [ ] The wheel over the strip still scrolls the document, and the label goes as it scrolls.
- [ ] A selection dragged from the text and let go beside a heading mark selects and goes nowhere.

## Decisions

- **The nearer mark wins; the lane only breaks a tie in one row.** A click or the pointer within 4 px (`MARK_REACH`) of a mark acts on the nearest one, a heading's measured from its mark's middle and a match's from its place. Only when the nearest heading mark and the nearest match are in the same pixel row (or exactly as far) does the lane decide: the heading's in the strip's left half, the match in the right half, as the spec's "Clicks" says ("heading marks win over match marks in the same row only when the click is in the heading lane"). So the mark under the pointer never loses to one a few pixels away: a right-half click on an h1's full-width mark goes to the heading though a match 2.7 px below is in reach, and a left-half click on the current match's full-width mark makes that match current though a heading's mark 4 px above is in reach. A left-half click near only a match's mark makes that match current, and a right-half click near only a heading's mark goes to the heading. Ticket 03's test "a click in the strip's left half beside a match mark does nothing" contradicted that and was removed; its replacement, "a click in the strip's left half beside a match mark with no heading mark near makes that match current", is in `overview-ruler-headings.test.ts`. Ticket 03's "a click on the strip while Find is closed does nothing" clicked row 0, where its document's h1 has a mark that is now clickable, so it clicks row 110 instead (a match's row while Find looks, no heading's) and is renamed "a click where a match of Find would have its mark does nothing while Find is closed".
- **A run of headings is the headings of one pixel row**, as ticket 01 draws them (one mark per row, the highest level's length): each heading mark keeps its row's headings in document order (a non-empty list), so a click goes to the first and the label names them. Headings in rows that touch keep their own marks rather than merging as Find's runs do: a document with a heading every few rows would otherwise become one bar, losing its structure, and the label would name the whole document. Heading marks and Find's matches are indexed by one per-row index (`RowIndex`): a heading mark by its middle (its row + `HEADING_MARK_HEIGHT` / 2, the stylesheet's 2 px), a match by its place, the nearest found the same way for both, the upper of equals, so a pointer move looks at 9 rows at most however many headings and matches the document has. Both are scaled one way, a block's top times the strip's height over the scroll height.
- **A heading click goes through `visitHeading`** with the run's first heading's id, as `links.ts` does for a heading link, so it opens closed sections, scrolls as a link does and joins History; the press is armed and prevented as in ticket 03 (a left press on any mark), so a selection dragged from the text and let go beside a heading mark does nothing.
- **No mark acts while the strip as drawn may be wrong**, a heading's or a match's, for a click or the hover: while Find has counted again and the strip hasn't redrawn yet (one frame), and while the list of headings isn't the one the strip was drawn from or no longer describes the view. During a reload the new render is in the view a landing before its list is, and the strip keeps the old document's marks until then (ticket 01), whose headings may have moved or gone: hovering them names nothing and clicking them goes nowhere, until the redraw from the new list.
- **The strip's place is read as it is drawn**, not at each event: `DrawnStrip` keeps the strip's top and left edge in the window, from the scroller's box in the redraw's read phase. The scroller moves only when its size changes (its inset is the title bar room), which redraws it, so a pointer move reads no layout. A move left of the strip costs one comparison. The click, which ticket 03 placed by the scroller's box read at the click, now uses the same kept place.
- **The hover label** is one element kept in the strip (so it takes no pointer, is `aria-hidden` and goes when the strip hides), `.overview-ruler-label`, in the look of `.corner-bubble` and `.link-target`, from one rule it shares with `.corner-bubble` in `document.css`, 4 px left of the strip. It runs down from its mark in the strip's upper half and up from it in the lower half, so it stays in the window without being measured. One line per heading, up to five, then "and N more". A heading without text (one of only an image) is left out rather than given an empty line, and a mark whose headings all have none shows no label, only the hand: its id is made for links, not for reading. It is rebuilt only when the pointer moves to another mark, or back onto its mark after a scroll took it away. It goes when the pointer leaves the mark or the scroller (`mouseleave`), on a scroll (the wheel over the strip scrolls the text under it), when another document is shown, on every redraw (the marks may have moved; the next move finds the mark again) and when the strip hides (switched off, a wait note, no scroll). No label or hand shows while a button is held, as during a selection drag or a drag of the scroll bar's thumb.
- **The hand** is the scroller's `cursor`, set only when the pointer comes onto or leaves a mark, since the strip takes no pointer: the content under the strip (the view's padding, or the scroller's margin in a wide window) inherits it. It shows over any mark a click acts on, Find's too. The change restyles the scroller's laid-out content once per entry or exit, not per move; ticket 05's measurement on the large documents can tell whether that is felt.
- **Files.** The placement moved from `overview-ruler.ts` to `overview-ruler-placement.ts` as a prefactor (tests green before the change), which then became one deep module: `placeStrip(shown, headings, matches)` measures the top-level blocks and returns a `PlacedStrip` (its heading marks, its match runs, `matchRow(i)` for the current match's mark, and `markAt(x, y)`, pure, with the lane rule inside), and keeps each count's places of matches in their blocks itself, so `overview-ruler.ts` holds only the DOM, the events and the lifecycle. The shared test helpers moved from `overview-ruler.test.ts` to `overview-ruler-test-support.ts`, so this ticket's tests sit in `overview-ruler-headings.test.ts` and every file stays well under 1,000 lines. The tests' fake of the webview's heading scrolls is `test-support.ts`'s `spyOnHeadingScrolls`, which with `{ laidOut: true }` also lays the page out at the scrolled-to line, rather than a copy per test file. No contract shape changed. The hand's cost is measured in ticket 05, not here.
