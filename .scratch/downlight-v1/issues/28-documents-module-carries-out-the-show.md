# 28: The documents module carries out the whole show

**What to build:** A refactor of the app, with no change the reader sees. `Documents::give` decides what a show does, and now also carries it out: opening the new window, titling a window given another document, bringing a window to the front, and undoing the show when part of it fails. The window side goes through a small port beside `Pages`, with Tauri in the app and the recorder in the tests, so the whole show is tested without a webview.

Spec: [Downlight v1](../spec.md)

**Blocked by:** none

**Status:** ready-for-human

- [x] A windows port (roughly: open a window, set its title, bring it to the front) sits beside `Pages`. Tauri's `AppHandle` is the app's adapter and the recorder the tests' adapter, as ticket 22 decided for `Pages`: a trait with two adapters, not code generic over `R: Runtime`, and no mock runtime.
- [x] `window::show`, `Shown`, `open_or_forget` and `links::left_for_the_page` fold into the documents module, or go; `links::follow` maps what `give` did to `Followed` directly.
- [x] What a new window starts with is decided in one place: the page URL and its flags (`timing`, `backdrop`), the navigation guard, the backdrop, the key setup and the 5 s fallback. `Timing::page_url` moves out of the timing module, which only times. Ticket 16 adds the zoom, size and offset there.
- [x] Ticket 22's open item 6: a give whose page can't be told restores the window's previous entry, so the table and the window agree. Tested with the recorder.
- [x] Ticket 22's open item 7: tests that read the table's internals (`wait_for_render_ahead`, `opening_an_open_document_again_starts_no_render_for_its_window`) check what the recorder sees instead; `watch_that_cannot_start_is_noticed_once_the_page_asks` proves the hold.
- [x] `open.rs`'s drop notice and `menu.rs`'s menu command go through the documents module's `Pages`, not straight through `AppHandle`, so the recorder sees every message a page gets.
- [x] Errors the reader sees no longer carry `tauri::Error`: a documents error type says what went wrong in the reader's terms. Today `Given::WindowGone` reaches the reader as "window not found", through `OpenError::Show`, `FollowError::Show` and `LaunchFailure`.

## Results

Built 2026-11-16. `npm run check` passes.

- **The windows port.** `Windows` (`open`, `set_title`, `bring_to_front`) sits in `app/src/documents.rs` beside `Pages`, and `Documents::new(pages, windows)` takes both. `impl Windows for AppHandle` in `app/src/window.rs` is the app's adapter; a closed window's `set_title` and `bring_to_front` are no failure, as `Pages::send` to a closed window isn't. The tests' `Recorder` moved from `message.rs` to `app/src/documents/recorder.rs` and adapts both ports: `sent()` as before, `done()` for `ToWindow::Opened(NewWindow)`, `Titled` and `BroughtToFront`, `fail_window(label)` beside `fail_sends_to`, and `recorded()` to make a `Documents` on it.
- **The whole show in `give`.** `window::show`, `Shown`, `open_or_forget`, `window::open_empty`, `links::show_document`, `destination` and `left_for_the_page` are gone. `claim` returns what to carry out (`Claimed::NewWindow`, `Changed { left, .. }`, `AlreadyHere`, `AlreadyElsewhere { scroll_to }`, `WindowGone`), and `give` opens, titles, tells and brings to the front, then starts the watch. `Given::WindowGone` is gone: a closed target is `Err(ShowError::WindowClosed)`. `Documents::open_empty` opens its window too. `title_of` moved to `documents.rs` beside `file_name_shown`. `links::follow` takes `&Documents` and maps `Given::AlreadyHere` to `Followed::ScrollTo`, anything else to `Followed::Nothing`. `open_launch` takes `&Documents`.
- **What a new window starts with.** `NewWindow` carries what the documents module decides per window (its title; ticket 16 adds the size and the offset), and `open_window` in `window.rs` stays the one place for what every window starts with alike: the page URL and its flags (`page_url`, out of `timing.rs` since window design ticket 01), the frame, the navigation guard, the backdrop, the key setup, the maximize button and the 5 s fallback.
- **Open item 6.** A change titles the window first, then tells its page; if either fails, `put_back` restores the entry the window left (unless the window was given another since, or closed) and the old title, and `give` fails with `ShowError::NotTaken`. A new window that can't open is forgotten (`ShowError::NotOpened`), and one that can't come to the front gives `ShowError::NotBroughtToFront`.
- **Errors in the reader's terms.** `ShowError` reads after the document's name: "its window has closed", "its window didn't open: ...", "the window couldn't change to it: ...", "its window didn't come to the front: ...". The system's own words come after the colon as `WindowError`, a string, which `Pages` and `Windows` fail with; nothing the reader sees carries `tauri::Error` from a show. `LaunchFailure` is an enum: `Can't show C:\docs\a.md: its window didn't open: ...`, or `Can't open a window: ...` for a launch that names no document. The launch's line used to read `Can't open a window for C:\docs\a.md: ...`.
- **Every message through `Documents`.** `Documents::send` is public, and the drop's notice (`open::show_dropped`), the menu's command (`menu::forward`) and the title bar's `MaximizeButton` and `WindowState` go through it rather than `AppHandle`, so `Pages` is no longer called anywhere else.

**Beyond the plan:**

- Dropping the entry a window leaves now happens after the table's lock, so its watch stops outside the lock.
- New tests through the recorder: in `documents/tests/give.rs`, a page that can't be told (the table, the showing's path and the title all go back), a window that can't be titled (its page isn't told, which shows the title goes first), a new window titled after its document, a window given a document retitled and its page told, a new or empty window that can't open is forgotten, an open document's window brought to the front (also the requesting one), one that can't come to the front, and a closed window's error text. In `links.rs`, `follow` itself: another document in this window, Ctrl+click opening a new window, a link to this window's document scrolling (also with Ctrl+click), one shown elsewhere scrolling that window and bringing it to the front, a window that can't open, and a link from a showing the window has left. These use the core's fixture documents, not a temporary folder: removing the folder raced with the watch starting and sent a "Can't watch" notice. In `open.rs`, a drop shows the document, a drop without Markdown sends its notice, a drop on a closed window says so. In `menu.rs`, a choice reaches its window's page. In `window/tests.rs`, launches with and without documents, and their failures.
- Tests changed: `window/tests.rs`'s launch failure tests build their failures through a launch with a failing window, since `LaunchFailure` holds a `ShowError` now, and their expected text changed as above; the two title tests became recorder tests (the drive root one stays on `title_of`). `document_given_to_a_window_whose_page_cannot_be_told_reports_the_failure` became `..._is_not_taken` and checks what the window keeps. `showing_in_a_closed_window_changes_nothing` checks `Err(ShowError::WindowClosed)` instead of `Given::WindowGone`. The tests of `destination` and `left_for_the_page` went with them.
- Open item 7: the recorder can't see a render, so the render-ahead tests (`window_given_a_new_document_renders_it_ahead`, `window_changed_to_another_document_renders_the_new_one_ahead`, `opening_an_open_document_again_starts_no_render_for_its_window`, `reload_for_the_shown_document_tells_the_page_and_reads_the_file_again`, `reload_overtaken_by_another_document_sends_nothing`) check when the handed-over read started, which the timing switch reports as `at` (`Stages::started`, test-only), against a moment 200 ms after the render could have started. A render never started ahead fails them on any machine; only a machine that takes over 200 ms to start a blocking thread could fail them wrongly. `wait_for_render_ahead` and the reads of `documents.lock()` are gone; the "no render" test also checks the recorder (opened, then brought to the front). The reload test no longer writes the file, since the window's real watch would report that write during the pause. `watch_that_cannot_start_is_noticed_once_the_page_asks` now requires the notice in the first answer and no message before or after, waiting 1 s for the watch to fail.

**Measured:** `npm run measure` on the release build (medians of 5, not against `HEAD` built the same way). No rendering work changed; what moved is the order of a show (the window opens, then the watch starts, where the watch used to start first) and that the window opens from `give`. The render still starts when the window is given its document: `readStart` is within 0.3 ms of `windowOpening` in every case. Cold first paint: 100 KB 787 ms (target under 1.0 s; ticket 02's baseline 771), 1 MB mixed 1137 ms, 1 MB prose 783 ms. A second window, 100 KB: 249 ms from the window opening (target under 300 ms), 339 ms from the second launch. A reload of 1 MB mixed: 882 ms from the save, insert 63 ms, anchor 66 ms, no lines moved, nothing over 100 ms after it. Find and scrolling had no frames over 100 ms after the document but one or two single ones while typing (102 and 104 ms worst), as before.

**Not yet checked by hand** (release build, built): the reader-visible parts that moved to new code paths. A launch with documents and one without; a second launch's document opening in the running process, and one already open coming to the front; Ctrl+O and dropping a file, both changing the window's title; dropping a non-Markdown file showing its notice; a link to another document, a heading link to a document open in another window (that window comes forward and scrolls) and a Ctrl+click; a right-click menu command; the title bar's maximize button hover and press, and maximize and restore swapping its icon (these messages now go through `Documents`).

## Comments

**2026-11-13, from the architecture review.** Following a link to a document runs through `Target`, `Claimed`, `Given`, `Shown`, `Followed` and `Place`; `Shown` only re-maps `Given` for one caller. The pure helpers around the orchestration are tested (`destination`, `left_for_the_page`, `dropped_document`, `item_id` and `chosen`), but their callers aren't, since they take a concrete `AppHandle`. Ticket 12's Back must retitle the window and ticket 16's new window takes a size and an offset; both become testable here.

**2026-11-16, from window design ticket 01.** Part of the third checkbox is done: `page_url` and `WindowLook` moved from `timing.rs` to `window.rs`, beside `open_window`, and `Timing` only says whether timing is on (`Timing::is_on`). The flags now include `titleBar` (the page draws the title bar in a frameless window), and they have a contract sample (`app/tests/fixtures/page_url.json`, every flag on and none), which the page's `pageFlags` in `page/src/app.ts` is checked against. What is left is gathering the rest of what a new window starts with in one place.

**2026-11-16, from ticket 29.** The key setup of a new window is now `platform::leave_keys_to_the_page(&window)` in `open_window` (`app/src/window.rs`), right after `build()`: it turns WebView2's browser keys off and adds the one `AcceleratorKeyPressed` handler. It returns nothing and never fails the window, so a windows port that records what a window starts with can record it as one step with no outcome.

**2026-11-16, from ticket 26.** `Documents::reload` and the `reload_document` command are gone (F5 asks the page's view directly), and `Documents::handover` sends nothing now: a held notice goes in the answer. Every message a page gets from the documents module is `AskForDocument`, `Gone(Showing)`, `ScrollToHeading` or `Notice`. `links::follow` looks the document up with `Documents::document_path_shown_as(window, showing)`, since `follow_link` now takes the showing whose document the link was in; folding `follow` into the documents module keeps that check.
